# Barcode Deck — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Barcode Deck stores your cards only on your device, in the app's own
database. It has no server, no account system, and no analytics, and it
does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** The app never asks you to sign in or register.
- **No tracking.** There are no analytics, no ads, no identifiers, and no
  third-party SDKs.
- **Camera.** The camera is used exclusively to recognize barcodes
  on-device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
- **Export & import.** Sharing happens only through files you explicitly
  create and share yourself (e.g. AirDrop, Messages, or the system share
  sheet). We never see those files.
- **No network.** The Android version does not request the Internet
  permission at all; the iOS version performs no network requests.

## Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data of any kind, and no third parties
receive any data from it.
